# MiniMax H3 · 动作迁移（参考视频换人）提示词模板

> **场景**：1 张人物图（你的角色）+ 1 段参考视频（别人做的动作）→ 产出「参考视频的动作 + 你图里的人物」。
> **依据**：官方 API 文档 `platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation`；官方 Full-Reference 指南 `ref-en.txt`。
> **结论先说**：官方**没有**单独的「换人 / 动作迁移」章节，但给出了明确机制，并且官方 r2va 代码示例用的就是这个用法。不用自己猜。

---

## 一、官方依据（四条，逐条可查）

**① 官方 API 示例的 prompt 字段（最直接的证据）**

```text
The character performs street dance, following the motion in reference video 1;
the character's appearance follows reference images 1 and 2.
```

对应 content：2 个 `reference_image` + 1 个 `reference_video`。这就是「换人 + 动作迁移」。

**② 同一个 Subject 可以由多份素材共同定义**（ref-en.txt §2.1）

```text
<Subject 1> is the woman whose appearance comes from <Picture 1> and whose walking motion comes from <Video 1>.
```

**③ 保留关系标记 `attribute_transfer`**（ref-en.txt §4.1）

> Referenced characteristics are transferred to a different identifiable target subject.
> （被参考的特征被转移到另一个可识别的目标主体上。）

**④ `<Video N>` 只留给整段视频关系**（ref-en.txt §2.3）

> If a person, object, scene, action, or effect from a reference video is reused as visible content,
> it still belongs under `<Subject N>`.

`<Video N>` 仅用于「剪辑源 / 续写起点 / 参考整段运镜·剪辑节奏·时序结构」。**只取其中一段动作，动作本身归 `<Subject N>`**，`<Video N>` 只作为出处被引用，不单列。

**⑤ 官方还提供 H3-Context-IR 接口**（`POST /v2/h3_context_ir`）
把 text + image + video + audio 丢进去，它先帮你把多模态关系解读成一份**结构化 prompt**（返回 `content.prompt`），你再拿去生成。**它只产出提示词，不出片。** 适合「我描述不清，让官方先帮我写六段」的场景。

## 二、素材怎么传（API 层）

| 你的素材 | `type` | `role` |
|---|---|---|
| 人物图（你的角色） | `image_url` | `reference_image` |
| 动作参考视频 | `video_url` | `reference_video` |
| 文本 prompt | `text` | —（**必填**） |

**硬约束**

- 数量：图片 ≤ 9、视频 ≤ 3、音频 ≤ 3，**合计 ≤ 12 个文件**
- 参考视频：单个 2–15 秒，总时长 ≤ 15 秒
- 单文件：视频 ≤ 50MB、图 ≤ 30MB、音频 ≤ 15MB；整个请求体 ≤ 64MB
- prompt ≤ 7000 字符；`duration` 4–15 整数；`ratio` 可选，默认 `adaptive`
- 格式：视频 MP4/MOV（H.264/H.265）；图 JPG/PNG/WEBP/HEIC/HEIF；音频 WAV/MP3
- 帧率 23.976–60；宽高比 0.4–2.5

⚠️ **首尾帧与参考素材互斥**：content 里一旦出现 `reference_image / reference_video / reference_audio`，就**不能**再出现 `first_frame / last_frame`（反之亦然）。想让你的图当「首帧」→ 走 i2va，用不了参考视频；要动作迁移 → 走 r2va，图只能当 `reference_image`。

## 三、六段模板（动作迁移专用，直接填空）

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the <你的角色是谁> in <Picture 1>, with <3~5 个关键特征，来自图>.
<Subject 2> is the <动作描述，如 armed-combat combo / slow walk-and-sit> motion from <Video 1>, transferred to <Subject 1>. Do not copy the performer, the original costume, the background, or the audio from <Video 1>.
<Subject 3> is the <场景，可选> from <Picture 2>, with <场景关键特征>.

summary:
[reference generation] The target video shows <Subject 1> performing <动作概述> in <场景>; <Picture 1> supplies identity and appearance, and <Video 1> supplies motion only.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - <逐条列出：脸型、发型、服装、身材比例>.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): attribute_transfer - only the <动作> motion and its timing transfer to <Subject 1>; the original performer's appearance, the background, and the camera framing from <Video 1> are not retained.
<Subject 3> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - <场景保留项>.

detailed_description:
<1~2 句全片风格：写实/电影感、光影、质感、画幅>.
[Shot 1] <景别> frames <Subject 1>, <首次出现时写参考特征 + 画幅位置>, <起始动作>. The camera <运镜类型 + 幅度 + 速度>. <该镜头的环境声或动作声>.
[Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the shot cuts to <景别>, <后续只用 <Subject 1> 标签 + 动作/运镜>. The shot ends on <明确的最后一帧状态>.

overall_soundscape:
<全片环境音 / 物理声 1~4 句：脚步、衣物摩擦、呼吸、环境底噪>.

non_diegetic_music:
<乐器 + 速度 + 动态 1~3 句；没有写 N/A>
```

**写模板时的三条纪律**

1. **动作抽出来当 Subject，不要把整段视频当 `<Video N>` 单列。** 单列会连演员、背景、声音一起带进来。
2. **`<Subject 2>` 那条必须带"不要复制"句**，而且要点名：performer / costume / background / audio / camera framing。
3. **主体首次出现写特征，后续镜头只写标签**，不重复长相描述。

## 四、完整示例（图 = 角色，视频 = 动作）

> 角色/场景为占位，替换成你自己的定妆图与场景即可。

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the young woman in <Picture 1>, with short black bob hair, the outfit shown in <Picture 1>, and a clean, gentle modern look.
<Subject 2> is the sharp three-move martial-arts combo performed in <Video 1> — a low sweep, a rising elbow, and a half-turn recovery — transferred to <Subject 1>. Do not copy the performer, the original costume, the background, the lighting, or the audio from <Video 1>.
<Subject 3> is the empty riverside promenade in <Picture 2>, with a stone railing, a broad walkway, and a city skyline on the far shore.

summary:
[reference generation] The target video shows <Subject 1> performing the <Subject 2> combat combo on <Subject 3>. <Picture 1> supplies her identity and appearance, <Picture 2> supplies the environment, and <Video 1> supplies motion only.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - her short black bob hair, facial identity, body proportions, and the outfit from <Picture 1> are retained.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): attribute_transfer - only the timing, body mechanics, and rhythm of the three-move combo transfer to <Subject 1>; the original performer's appearance, costume, background, and audio are not retained.
<Subject 3> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - the stone railing, walkway, and far-shore skyline are retained.

detailed_description:
The target video uses a realistic, cinematic urban-action look with cool late-afternoon light, subtle film grain, natural skin texture, no subtitles, and no extra text.
[Shot 1] A medium-wide shot frames <Subject 1>, the short-haired young woman in the outfit from <Picture 1>, standing at the center of <Subject 3>, the riverside promenade with its stone railing and far-shore skyline. She drops into a low sweep, her weight shifting onto the front leg as her coat flares. The camera trucks right with small amplitude at slow speed, keeping her centered. Her foot brushes the pavement once.
[Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the shot cuts to a tighter medium shot as <Subject 1> rises out of the sweep into a sharp elbow strike and a half-turn recovery, her hair swinging across her face on the turn. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed and settles as she lands in a steady stance facing camera. She holds the stance for the final second.

overall_soundscape:
Faint riverside ambience continues throughout: distant traffic, occasional birdsong, and light water movement near the railing. Her footsteps, coat rustle, and one sharp exhale land on the strikes.

non_diegetic_music:
A low percussive pulse at a slow tempo, tightening into a single short accent on the elbow strike, then dropping out under the final hold.
```

## 五、四个常见坑

1. **把参考视频笼统写成 `<Video 1>` 并要求"参考它"。** → 演员、背景、运镜、声音会被一起带进来。只取动作就抽成 Subject。
2. **忘了写"不复制什么"。** 官方要求显式声明参考关系；不写就会被模型自由发挥。
3. **混用首尾帧和参考素材。** 接口直接报参数错误。想锁首帧 → i2va；想动作迁移 → r2va。
4. **动作太长 / 参考视频超过 15 秒。** 总时长 ≤ 15 秒，超了要先剪。

---

## 六、一句话流程

1. 人物图 → `reference_image`
2. 动作视频（剪到 ≤15s）→ `reference_video`
3. 按其上六段写 prompt：**动作抽成 `<Subject N>` + `attribute_transfer` + "不要复制原演员"句**
4. `duration` 4–15 整数，`ratio` 可留 adaptive
5. 拿不准提示词怎么写 → 先打 H3-Context-IR，拿它返回的结构化 prompt 再生成
