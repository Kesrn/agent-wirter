# H3 参考视频不跟随 · 排查与修法

> **现象**：给了 `@图片1/@图片2` + `@视频1`，出片动作不按参考视频走，模型自己重编一整套。
> **依据**：官方 API 文档（r2va 约束）+ 官方 `ref-en.txt`。
> **标注约定**：【官方口径】= 文档白纸黑字；【推断】= 我的判断，需实测确认。

---

## 一、先做三件事，可能根本不用改提示词

### ① 确认参考视频真的传进去了

打开你的请求体，看 `content` 数组里有没有这一条：

```json
{"type": "video_url", "video_url": {"url": "..."}, "role": "reference_video"}
```

**如果 `@视频1` 没被前端解析成 `role=reference_video`，模型手里就只有文字** —— 那它只能自己编，跟提示词写得多好没关系。这是第一嫌疑。

### ② 时长与画幅必须和参考视频对齐

| 情况 | 会发生什么 |
|---|---|
| 源视频 15s，你生成 10s | 模型必须**压缩**动作 → 只能重编 |
| 源视频 8s，你生成 10s | 模型必须**补 2 秒** → 自己编 |
| 源视频竖屏 9:16，你出 16:9 | 构图必须**重构** → 整片像重做 |

**做法**：`duration` 设成与源视频相同的整数秒；`ratio` 设成与源视频一致。官方约束：参考视频单个 **2–15 秒**、总时长 ≤ 15 秒 —— 超了会被截，也会导致动作对不上。

### ③ 参考视频本身有没有超 15 秒

超过 15 秒的部分不会进入参考。先剪到你要的那一段。

## 二、提示词里的四个真原因

### 🔴 D. 最隐蔽也最致命：你在正文里自己编了动作

原文：

```
they step outward into mirrored eight-count footwork ... they cross and swap places
without touching ... they land together on a synchronized two-handed arm gesture
```

**这些动作不是从参考视频里抄的，是你凭想象写的。** 模型现在收到两套互相矛盾的信号：

1. 参考视频（真实编舞）
2. 你正文里写的新编舞

而**文字指令的权重通常压过参考素材** —— 于是它演的是"你编的那套"，看起来就是"自己重新生成"。

**两条出路，选一条：**

- **出路 A（推荐，省事）**：正文**不再自编动作**，只写「按 `<Subject 2>` 复现 + 换身体 + 场景/镜头关系」。动作细节全部交给 `<Subject 2>` 和参考视频。
- **出路 B（要逐拍控制）**：把参考视频**逐拍抄成拍点表**再写进正文。必须真的对着视频数拍子，不能凭印象。

### 🔴 E. 没给 `<Video 1>` 的时序锚点

你现在只声明了 `<Subject 2>`（动作），还额外写了 `do not copy ... camera framing`。

【官方口径】`<Video N>` 的用途之一就是 *"Referencing the original video's camera movement, cuts, rhythm, or **temporal structure**"*（ref-en.txt §2.3）。

也就是说：**你等于主动告诉模型"整段结构和节奏你自己发挥"**。它当然重编。

**改法**：把参考视频的**时序结构**单列成 `<Video 1>`，并给它自己的 retention 行：

```text
<Video 1> (temporal structure): fully_preserved - the target video follows the full
timeline of <Video 1> without adding, removing, reordering, or re-timing any beat.
```

【推断】同一份素材同时作为 `<Subject 2>` 的出处和独立的 `<Video 1>` 行，依据 §2.3「`<Video N>` identifies the asset or structural source and does not replace subject labels」的措辞是允许的 —— 文档没给这个组合的现成例子，值得实测。

### 🟠 F. "do not copy" 清单太长太宽

你写了：`do not copy the performers, the original costumes, the background, the lighting setup, or the audio`，正文风格句里又加了不保留运镜。

结果是**模型手里一个空间锚、一个镜头锚都没有** —— 背景自己造、灯光自己打、镜头自己设计、节奏自己定。四条全松开，出来就是一支新片。

**改法**：只否掉真正要换的两项（**人**和**服装**），其余改成"保留/对齐"的正向写法：

- 背景 → 用 `<Subject 4>` 棚景图钉死（**正向锚**）
- 灯光 → `the key light and colour grade follow <Video 1>`（对齐，不是否决）
- 运镜 → 想严格跟动作，**镜位最好也跟源一致**。换了镜位，同一个动作看起来也完全不同。

### 🟠 G. 动作定义停在摘要级

`<Subject 2>` 只列了四个动作名（walk-in / eight-count / partnered turns / sunglasses reveal）。官方 §5.3 要求标签在首次出现处**描述实际可见的**内容。舞蹈这种纯时序内容，摘要级定义等于没定义。

**改法**：至少写到「身体部位 + 方向 + 拍点」，例如 `three steps right with the torso held upright, then a half-turn on the right heel on beats 5-6`。

## 三、修正版（变体 A：正文不再自编动作）

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the adult woman in <Picture 1>, with violet eyes, a long dark purple-black braided ponytail reaching waist level, an oversized golden leaf-shaped hair ornament, a plum-purple and lavender layered gown with gold embroidery and off-shoulder sheer sleeves, and a regal, elegant appearance.
<Subject 2> is the complete dance performance in <Video 1> — the exact body mechanics, footwork, gesture timing, torso and head orientation, partnered spatial arrangement, and prop interaction across its entire duration. The motion, its order, and its timing are reproduced as-is; only the performers' bodies are replaced by <Subject 1> and <Subject 3>.
<Subject 3> is the young man in <Picture 2>, with tousled auburn-red hair, amber-brown eyes, a white hooded zip-up jacket worn open over a mustard-yellow crew T-shirt, a black cord necklace with a spiral pendant, black slim-fit pants, and black lace-up boots.
<Subject 4> is the studio set in <Picture 3>, including the hot-pink floor and backdrop, the red drum kit, the black guitar amplifiers, the microphone stand, and the black sunglasses used in the closing beat.

summary:
[reference generation] A [源视频时长]-second 16:9 music-video segment in which <Subject 1> and <Subject 3> reproduce the <Subject 2> choreography inside <Subject 4>, following <Video 1>'s timeline. <Picture 1> and <Picture 2> supply the two performers' identities and appearances, <Picture 3> supplies the set, and <Video 1> supplies motion, timing, and temporal structure only.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - the violet eyes, the long braided ponytail, the golden leaf ornament, and the plum-purple embroidered gown with sheer sleeves are retained from <Picture 1>.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1]): attribute_transfer - the entire choreography is reproduced faithfully and beat-for-beat onto <Subject 1> and <Subject 3>: same order, same timing, same spatial arrangement between the two bodies, same prop interaction. Only the performers' bodies are replaced. The original performers' faces, hair, and costumes are not retained.
<Subject 3> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - the auburn-red hair, amber eyes, white hooded jacket, yellow T-shirt, spiral pendant, black pants, and boots are retained from <Picture 2>.
<Subject 4> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - the pink floor and backdrop, red drum kit, black amplifiers, microphone stand, and sunglasses are retained from <Picture 3>.
<Video 1> (temporal structure and framing): fully_preserved - the target video follows the complete timeline of <Video 1> without adding, removing, reordering, or re-timing any beat, and keeps the same shot size, camera position, and camera movement as <Video 1>.

detailed_description:
The target video uses a bright, high-energy pop music-video look with even studio lighting, natural skin texture, realistic fabric movement, and a landscape 16:9 composition. There is no dialogue and no on-screen text.
[Shot 1] A single continuous shot reproduces <Subject 2>, the full dance performance from <Video 1>, with <Subject 1>, the plum-gowned woman with the waist-length braid from <Picture 1>, and <Subject 3>, the auburn-haired young man in the open white jacket from <Picture 2>, replacing the two source performers. The choreography, its order, its timing, and the spatial relationship between the two dancers follow <Video 1> exactly; no step, gesture, turn, or prop interaction is added, skipped, or re-sequenced. <Subject 1> and <Subject 3> stand inside <Subject 4>, the hot-pink studio with the red drum kit and black amplifiers, and the shot size, camera position, and camera movement match <Video 1> throughout. The gown's sheer sleeves and hem, and <Subject 1>'s braid, follow the motion of the original bodies and trail a half-beat behind each movement. Fabric snaps and footsteps land on the same beats as <Video 1>.

overall_soundscape:
Bright studio room tone continues throughout the video: footsteps on the pink floor, the rustle and snap of the gown and jacket, one soft cloth impact as the two cross, and the performers' audible breathing.

non_diegetic_music:
An up-tempo rock track at a fast tempo with driving drums, distorted electric guitar, and a steady bass line, entering on the first beat and cutting off cleanly on the final frame.
```

**相比上一版改了四处**

| 改动 | 原因 |
|---|---|
| 正文删掉所有自编动作，改成「复现 + 换身体 + 对齐源」 | 消除"你编的动作"压过参考视频 |
| 新增独立的 `<Video 1>` 行 + `fully_preserved` 时序声明 | 给出时序/镜位锚点 |
| 只否掉 faces/hair/costumes，背景灯光运镜改成"对齐源" | 别把模型的锚点全撤掉 |
| `<Subject 2>` 定义从四个动作名扩成"逐拍复现"的明确声明 | 摘要级 → 可执行 |

## 四、如果还不够，上拍点表（变体 B）

正文里**每一条动作都必须是你能在参考视频里指着说的**。格式：

```text
[Shot 1] A single continuous shot. From 00:00 to 00:02, <Subject 1> takes three steps
toward camera-right with her torso upright, weight on the right leg, and <Subject 3>
mirrors her on the opposite foot. From 00:02 to 00:04, both pivot a quarter turn to the
left on the outer heel, ...
```

**注**：`[Shot N] At MM:SS.mmm` 的时间戳按官方规则是从**第 2 个镜头**才开始用。单镜头内的时间用自然语言（`from 00:00 to 00:02`）表达即可。

如果你愿意把参考视频发我，我可以直接帮你把它逐拍抄成这张表 —— 凭印象写一定会编。

## 五、预期管理（重要）

**⚠️【推断，非官方口径】H3 的动作参考是语义级引导，不是 pose 级驱动。**

官方文档的描述是 *"Reference character, motion, camera, style, voice, or editing rhythm"* 和 *"keeps the features of the reference subject or asset consistent"* —— **官方从未承诺逐帧或逐拍精确复刻动作**。

所以现实的预期是：

- ✅ 能达到：动作**风格与段落节奏**接近、人物外观一致、空间关系合理
- ❓ 不确定：每一拍都对得上、手部细节一致
- ❌ 别指望：像 pose-transfer 专用模型（AnimateAnyone / Wan-Animate 那类骨骼驱动）那样 1:1 复刻

如果你要的是严格 1:1 动作复刻，H3 可能不是对的工具 —— 先按上面改完试一次，如果还是明显跑偏，就该考虑 pose 驱动的方案了。

---

## 六、一次只改一个变量

官方建议：**依次检查，哪项失败改哪一句**。整段推倒重写，下次即使变好，你也不知道是哪条起作用。

建议顺序（每步单独出片对比）：

1. 只确认 `role=reference_video` 传对了 + 时长画幅对齐 → 先出一版
2. 只加 `<Video 1>` 时序行 → 再出一版
3. 只删正文自编动作 → 再出一版
4. 还不行 → 上拍点表

这样 3–4 次就能定位到真正的瓶颈。
